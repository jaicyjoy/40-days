Causes of Curses & Curse Deliverance Prayer

Use this family-tree deliverance prayer together with prayer for your ancestors.

Curse deliverance: Ideally, after making a complete confession, pray this family-tree deliverance prayer for 21 days with fasting and prayer. It is best to recite it each day while participating in Holy Mass.

Causes of curses

  1. Idolatry — making an idol or worshipping it (Deuteronomy 27:15; Exodus 20:5)
  2. Dishonoring father and mother (Deuteronomy 27:16)
  3. Harming a neighbor (Deuteronomy 27:17)
  4. Cruelty toward persons with disabilities (Deuteronomy 27:18)
  5. Neglecting orphans (Deuteronomy 27:19)
  6. Fornication / sexual immorality (Deuteronomy 22:21–29)
  7. Sexual relations with blood relatives (incest) (Deuteronomy 27:20, 22–23)
  8. Sexual relations with animals (Deuteronomy 27:21)
  9. Adultery (Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Job 24:15–18; Numbers 5:27; Leviticus 20:10)
  10. Homosexual acts (Genesis 19; Leviticus 20:13)
  11. Other sexual sins (Leviticus 20:13–14; Deuteronomy 22:25; 23:2)
  12. Keeping cursed objects (Deuteronomy 7:25–26; Joshua 7:11–12; Acts 19:19)
  13. Astrology, sorcery, omens, witchcraft, consulting the dead, and similar evil practices (Deuteronomy 18:9–13; Leviticus 20:6, 27)
  14. Murder (Deuteronomy 27:24)
  15. Consenting to murder (including abortion) (Deuteronomy 27:25)
  16. Forsaking the Lord (Deuteronomy 28:20)
  17. Forgetting the Lord in prosperity (Deuteronomy 28:47–48)
  18. Not honoring the name of the Lord (Deuteronomy 28:58)
  19. Pride that ignores God’s Word and walks in one’s own way (Deuteronomy 29:19–20)
  20. Cursing the descendants of Abraham (Genesis 12:3; 27:29; Numbers 24:9)
  21. Refusing to work for the Lord (Judges 5:23; Jeremiah 48:10)
  22. Not giving glory to God (Malachi 2:2)
  23. Withholding offerings / tithes (Malachi 3:8–9; Haggai 1:2–11)
  24. Leading others away from the Lord into other religions (Deuteronomy 13:1–5, 13; 29:18)
  25. Taking away from or adding to the Word of God (Revelation 22:18–19)
  26. Teaching rebellion against the Lord (Jeremiah 28:16–17)
  27. Failing to warn those who sin (Ezekiel 3:18–21)
  28. Profaning the Sabbath / Lord’s Day (Exodus 31:14; Numbers 15:32–36)
  29. Distorting the Gospel (Galatians 1:8–9)
  30. Cursing rulers / those in authority (1 Kings 2:8–9; Exodus 22:28)
  31. Refusing to forgive others (Matthew 18:34–35)
  32. Child sacrifice (e.g. abortion) (Deuteronomy 18:10; Leviticus 18:21)
  33. Disobeying the commandments of the Lord (Deuteronomy 11:28; 27:26)

34. Curses that come through words

Death and life are in the power of the tongue (Proverbs 18:21). Words have power to bless or to curse (Mark 11:14; John 11:43). In the same way, our words can bless or curse, tear down or build up.

Curse-words spoken against us by others, curse-words we spoke against others, and curse-words we spoke against ourselves — forgive and renounce them (Matthew 5:44; 12:36).

An undeserved curse does not alight (Proverbs 26:2; Numbers 23:8; Genesis 27:13; Matthew 27:24–25; Numbers 14:27–28).

Curse Deliverance Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, I believe that You are the Son of God and the only way to God, and that You died on the Cross for my sins and rose again.

Lord Jesus, I turn from all my sins and turn to You for mercy and forgiveness. Father, if any of the ancestors named here, or their forebears, left this world without repenting of their sins and turning from them, O Jesus, as representatives of my family I (and my spouse) ask forgiveness for them and pray for them.

By the precious Blood that flowed from Your holy wounds, forgive their sins and debts. We confess and ask forgiveness for ourselves and for them regarding idolatry, demonic worship, rites to spirits, occult rituals, sorcery, witchcraft, black magic, harm done to the poor and helpless, withholding wages of workers, wrongs done to parents, killing of priests, killing of teachers, murder, abortion, unjust seizure of wealth, denying food, drinking water, pathways, shelter, and medical care, fornication, adultery, homosexual acts, bestiality, and other sexual sins. Grant eternal rest to those who have died; bless the living to live in holiness and attain eternal life.

By the Blood of Jesus, remove from our family tree every uncleanness and curse that entered through the sins and failures of our ancestors. In the name of Jesus we tear down every demonic altar built through covenants our ancestors made with the devil—directly or through sin. In Jesus’ name we declare that Satan and his angels have no further legal claim over our family lines. Confessing Jesus as Savior, God as Father, and the Holy Spirit as Guardian, we build an altar to the Triune God in our family.

Thus may every satanic door that passed into later generations be completely closed by the Blood of Jesus. By the power of Jesus’ name, may every claim of Satan over us be cancelled here and now. May every door needed for our spiritual life and growth, and every faculty of the soul for communion with God, be opened. I sprinkle the Blood of Jesus over myself and my family members, over my body, mind, and spirit, and over my home, health, wealth, and ministries for God. I declare the sovereignty of Jesus’ Blood over them. In my spiritual authority I command every serpent-spirit of poverty, failure, physical and mental illness, bad habits, disaster, and envy to leave my family and my life. May every fortress Satan raised over my life, God’s plans, and my family collapse in Jesus’ name. May the Kingdom of God be built up in me and in my loved ones.

O God, You who carry the key of the house of David upon Your shoulder: what You shut, no one can open; what You open, no one can shut (Isaiah 22:22).
